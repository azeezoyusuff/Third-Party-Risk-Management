# Third-Party-Risk-Management: Conducting a Security Assessment on Horizon Labs as a Supplier

## Purpose

To Conduct a Security Assessment on Horizon Labs as a Supplier and present executive risk summary; strategic risks that affect client’s ability to innovate, protect IP, and maintain regulatory compliance

## Goal

To present recommendations/a clear, executive‑level summary of the key risks that the client' senior management must be made aware of, based on Horizon Labs’ questionnaire responses.

## My Role

As a Cyber Security GRC Analyst, I was tasked to presents findings from the client’s Vendor assessment questionnaire on July 17, 2026, based directly on Horizon Labs’ questionnaire responses.
